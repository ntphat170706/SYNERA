## Platform

Web portal for NSX and NPP; separate Expo Sales app shares the backend.

## Product and users

SYNERA joins distribution operations and CRM: an order exception must connect to the customer, responsible people, follow-up commitments and retained outcomes. NSX manages Sales. NPP handles inbound purchasing and fulfilment to assigned stores; an operational NPP scope does not make Sales an NPP employee.

## Stack and constraints

Keep existing React 18/Vite web, Java 25 Spring Boot modular monolith and MySQL. Preserve backend authority, financial snapshots, scope, idempotency and existing Sales mobile compatibility. Keep original branding and shared tokens. No deletion of user documents or business data.

## Current delivery

User approved demonstrating core value before deep AI training and integrations, then clarified that scenarios are acceptance cases, never fixed frontend data. Build missing backend contracts and connect live data across all three clients. New sales replacement documents reserve/ship stock without a second charge; customer follow-up and notifications persist in the shared backend. Current NPP scope and traceability: docs/npp-portal/README.md.

## Success

A distributor can see work to handle, select and order products, track distinct quantities, and demonstrate how a shortage triggers NPP resolution plus Sales follow-up without a second charge. Do not claim actual model inference or production readiness from a prototype.

## Distributor portal refinements

The distributor only handles goods of participating manufacturers/brands that authorize it. Independent SKU ownership and brand-to-distributor grants are still a backend gap; UI scope and price scopes do not establish complete brand isolation.

The portal prioritizes daily tasks with direct entry points for orders, Excel, assistance and replenishment. Keep the original navy navigation and neutral white ground, with meaningful teal for stock, amber for attention, violet for customer follow-up/assistance and red for exceptions. Remove redundant identity strips and manual refresh controls; retain automatic refresh, visible failures and retry. Use dense full-width worklists, balanced secondary panels and compact mobile summaries. Do not place feature marketing or generic how-to prose inside operating screens.

Confirmed 2026-11-13: the left NPP navigation uses a light-to-deep blue ramp and six main areas; data surfaces remain flat. Outlet profiles and order details replace the list while open. Longer NPP forms and image evidence use right-side sheets; short tasks use compact dialogs, and store creation has two actual steps. Branding uses the full supplied transparent wordmark directly on the blue navigation, without a white plaque or typed substitute. Reference CloudGO outlet profiles for structure, not unsupported field or feature claims.

## Confirmed product direction and CRM 5in1

The manufacturer or brand is SYNERA's paying customer. A distributor participates under that brand's plan and explicit grants, and may retain another system for its wider business. Prioritize the user's latest feedback and the specification dated 12 November over the earlier small-distributor standalone positioning. Scope extends to the brand's authorized catalog, pricing, stock, transactions, reports and attachments; the current backend isolation gap remains open.

CRM 5in1 is the selected functional reference: CloudX (marketing, sales and care), CloudWORK (tasks and coordination), CloudCHECKIN (workforce and attendance), CloudEVENT (customer/partner events), and G-Connect (partner connections). Shared outlet/contact data, assigned people, commitments and transaction outcomes connect these groups to DMS. CloudX and CloudWORK must be evident in the core workflow. The complete workforce, events and community capabilities are not yet implemented merely because directory, visits or outbox endpoints exist. NSX manages its Sales workforce; the distributor coordinates fulfilment.

Screen composition is flexible: preserve these responsibilities while reducing empty or duplicate tables. The product's defining outcome is a distribution result becoming a care task with a responsible person and a deadline, then returning its resolution to the shared customer history. Do not replace this with disconnected CRUD modules or add five empty CRM menus. Source reconciliation and remaining contracts: docs/npp-portal/10-dinh-vi-crm5-dms.md.
